# Changelog

## ggsegCIT168 0.0.0.9000

- Added
  [`cit168()`](https://ggsegverse.github.io/ggsegCIT168/reference/cit168.md),
  the CIT168 probabilistic subcortical atlas (Pauli et al. 2018): 14
  structures per hemisphere covering the striatum, pallidum and the
  midbrain and diencephalic nuclei, drawn in two coronal and two axial
  views on the cortical ribbon, with 3D meshes.
